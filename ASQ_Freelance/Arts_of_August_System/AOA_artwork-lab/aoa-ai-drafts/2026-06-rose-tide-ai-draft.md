## 3. Visual Interpretation

Rose and violet light moves across the sky in loose, layered strokes, softening into lavender-blue near the horizon. Low island shapes sit in dark silhouette across the water, while the foreground is held by rocky shoreline forms in charcoal, slate, muted brown, and small warm ochre marks.

The scene feels connected to Scarborough and the Maine coast through atmosphere rather than exact detail. The water is quiet but not empty. The sky carries most of the warmth, while the rocks and islands give the piece its weight.

## 5. Description Draft

Rose light settles across the tide as the shoreline begins to darken.

This 12x12 original oil painting captures a quiet coastal evening in Scarborough, Maine, with violet clouds, pink reflection, and low island forms stretching across the water. Dark rocks hold the foreground, giving weight to the softness of the sky above.

The piece stays close to the feeling of the Maine coast at the edge of day: open water, changing light, and the last color held briefly on the surface.
