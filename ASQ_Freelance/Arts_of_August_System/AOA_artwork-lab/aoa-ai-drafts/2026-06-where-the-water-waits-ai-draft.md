## 15. Description

The water moves quietly through the marsh beneath a broad, layered sky. Pale channels catch the available light and turn through the darker land in long, unhurried curves. Above them, cloud cover gathers in slate blue, lavender-gray, and softened white, with small areas of muted blush where the light begins to shift.

The painting stays close to the stillness of the landscape. Deep green and charcoal marsh shapes hold the lower half of the composition, while the lighter water creates a calm path through the scene. Loose brushwork keeps the surface visible and grounded, allowing the changing light to carry the atmosphere.

This original oil painting is created on gallery-wrapped canvas and measures 11" x 14". The edges are finished for an unframed presentation. One of a kind. Price: $249.
