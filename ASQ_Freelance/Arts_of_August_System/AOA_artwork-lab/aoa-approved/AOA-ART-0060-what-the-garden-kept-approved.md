## 10. Description

The background is a deep, settled violet — the kind that arrives when the afternoon starts to cool and the garden gets quiet. Against it, a terracotta pot holds a full cluster of pink blooms, loose and open, painted with the kind of gestural marks that suggest movement without wind.

The light comes from low and to the side, warming the pot and the pale surface it rests on into amber and cream. The foliage layers between deep green and yellow-green, visible in quick, honest strokes that don't try to be more than they are.

The painting holds one specific moment — the last of the warm before the evening arrives.
