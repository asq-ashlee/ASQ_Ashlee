## Approved description

A weathered red-orange boat rests quietly in a field, surrounded by tall grass and Queen Anne's lace. The painting holds the contrast between a vessel shaped by years on the water and the landscape slowly growing around it, finding beauty in what has been weathered, well-loved, and left behind.

## Approved story

An old boat rests among tall grass and Queen Anne's lace, still carrying the suggestion of every place it once traveled.

## Artist story source

The artist described wondering about the stories old boats could tell: a boat no longer on the water becoming part of the landscape, resting among tall grass and Queen Anne's lace. The supplied story emphasizes the places it may have traveled, the people who climbed aboard, and the beauty in things that are weathered, well-loved, and left behind. The artist stated that the work is a 20x16 gallery-wrapped original and was available for sale when the story was posted as Day 14 of the 31 Days of Paintings series.
